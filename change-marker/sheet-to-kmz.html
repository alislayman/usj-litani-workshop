Sheet to KMZ

Turns your group's sheet into a map file for Google Earth (KMZ) and a GeoJSON file. It runs on your computer only: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. In Google Sheets, open your group's sheet and choose File, Download, Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  2. Drop the downloaded file in the box below (or click the box and choose it).
  3. Check the list of rows, then press Save KMZ and open the file in Google Earth.

How the Shape column is read

Each row is drawn from its Shape cell. The cell can hold any of these:

  • the line Change Marker copies with Copy for the sheet;
  • the text copied from geojson.io (a FeatureCollection, a Feature or a geometry);
  • a WKT shape such as POLYGON((35.51 33.31, 35.52 33.31, 35.52 33.32, 35.51 33.31));
  • a shape copied from Google Earth Pro (KML text with <coordinates>);
  • a point as 33.3157, 35.5127 (latitude, longitude, as Google Maps copies it).

If the Shape cell is empty but the row has Latitude and Longitude columns, the row becomes a point. Rows whose ID starts with "Example" are left out unless you untick the box above.
