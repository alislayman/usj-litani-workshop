How Change Marker works

  1. Pictures. Pick a village, a layer and two dates. Or dropDrop your own two pictures of the same place on two dates: screenshots, downloads, or a KMZ.
  2. Line them up. Our village pictures already are. Your own: pressPress Line up automatically, then check with the Checkerboard view: roads and field edges must run straight across the squares.
  3. Put on the map. Our village pictures already are. Your own: pinPin 3 places you can also find on Google Maps, far apart and not in a line.
  4. Compare. Side by side, Swipe (drag the divider), Blink, Fade or Checkerboard.
  5. Mark. Pick a kind at the top, then draw: Area, Line (roads and tracks) or Point. Write what you saw.
  6. Date. With the mark selected, step the After picture back month by month until the change is gone, then forward one: stop on the first picture that shows it and press First seen on this After picture. The tool writes that picture's date and the one before it. With your own pictures, UseWith the mark selected, Use the dates on screen writes the dates under Taken on.
  7. Export. KMZ for Google Earth, GeoJSON for geojson.io, and a CSV list of your marks to keep. For the group sheet, Copy for the sheet gives one mark's line to paste in its Shape cell.

Each mark stays on its ground: open another village whose pictures cover the same place and the mark is shown there too. A mark on pictures that are not on the map shows only on its own pictures.

What the layers show

  • True colour: as the eye sees it. Best for buildings, roads and bare ground.
  • False colour: plants show red. A forest, orchard or field that lost its plants turns grey, blue-green or brown.
  • Short-wave infrared: plants show green, fresh burn scars dark red to black, bare soil and rubble pink or pale.

What a picture cannot tell

A pair of pictures shows where the ground changed, and between which two dates. It does not show why, how, or who did it: only a source can say that. Drafts from Find changes are guesses: shadows, harvests, ploughing, haze and new leaves trigger them too. Keep only what you checked.

Keys

V select, H pan (or hold Space with the pointer on a picture), P area, F freehand, R box, L line, M point. Enter or double-click finishes, Esc cancels, Backspace removes the last corner while drawing (it never deletes a mark then), Del deletes the selected mark, and so does Backspace (delete on a Mac) with the Select tool. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, wheel to zoom, 0 fit, C copies the coordinates under the pointer, [ and ] move the swipe divider.

Your pictures and marks stay on this computer. Marks are kept in this browser between visits until you start fresh; use Save work to keep a file.
