Photo agencies

Professional news photos of the war in Lebanon. All are paid and for editorial use: check the licence before using any picture.

AgencyCoverageLicensing and access
AFP Forum Agence France-PresseExtensive Beirut bureau; continuous daily coverage of airstrikes, displacement and border clashes.Strictly paid commercial or editorial licensing. Requires a registered business or media account.
AP Newsroom Associated PressReal-time breaking photos, frontline damage and civilian impact across Lebanon and northern Israel.Paid licensing per image or by subscription. Editorial use only; no commercial or advertising use.
Reuters Connect ReutersDaily photo feeds in high volume from Beirut and the southern border towns, plus video stills.Paid enterprise or media licensing through Reuters Connect.
Anadolu Images Anadolu AgencyHeavy daily coverage from stringers in South Lebanon, Beirut and regional hubs.Paid registration required. Editorial and news use only, with mandatory credit.
EPA Images European Pressphoto AgencyA daily European and Middle Eastern photojournalism network with stringers in Lebanon.Paid rights-managed editorial licensing, priced by use, through EPA or its regional partners.
Getty Images Image libraryGathers the daily wire feeds, AFP and Anadolu among them, and independent conflict photographers.Pay per image or buy subscription packs. Easier for small blogs or independent creators to buy one editorial licence without a full wire contract.

Keywords

The words on screen in the tools and the sheet.

KeywordMeaning
Sentinel-2European satellites; a picture every few days, one pixel is 10 m.
True colour True colorAs the eye sees it: buildings, roads, bare ground.
False colour (plants red) False colorPlants show red; ground that lost its plants turns grey, blue-green or brown.
Short-wave infrared (burns) SWIRPlants green, fresh burn scars dark red to black, bare soil and rubble pale; one pixel is 20 m.
Before AfterThe two pictures you compare; the After picture steps month by month.
First seen on this After pictureWrites a mark's two dates: the picture before this one, and this one.
Seen betweenThe last picture without the change and the first with it.
Copy for the sheetPuts one mark on the clipboard, for the Shape (GeoJSON) cell.
Shape (GeoJSON)The mark's place, as one line of text.
KMZ for Google EarthSaves every mark with both pictures, for Google Earth.
Save workKeeps your marks in a file, in case the browser closes.
Find changesDrafts possible changes; shadows, harvests and haze set it off too. Keep a draft only after checking it.
Your own picturesCompare two pictures of your own, such as two screenshots.
Building destroyed Bulldozed land New road or track Burn scar White phosphorus Trees or plants lost Other changeThe kinds of mark in Change Marker.
Detonations Bulldozing Fires White phosphorus Other changesThe tabs of the sheet, one per kind.
TimelineThe tab that counts your rows per interval.
Evidence Seen and reported Seen only Reported by 2+ sources Reported by 1 sourceSeen on the pictures, in sources, or both.
Date (Beirut)The day a source gives, Beirut time.
Sources and mediaEvery link, the first post first; saved files by name.
NASA FIRMSHeat spots seen by satellites; a spot is a pixel 375 m to 1 km wide.
UTCWorld time. Beirut is UTC + 3 from 2026-03-29 to 2026-10-24, UTC + 2 before and after.
EFFISBurnt areas mapped by Copernicus from satellites.
haHectare: 100 m by 100 m.
GeoJSONA text format for places on a map.
PadletThe workshop's board, a column per group.
