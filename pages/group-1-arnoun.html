Group 1

Arnounأرنون

On the plateau next to Beaufort Castle, about 1.3 km from the Litani gorge.

Three roles

  • PicturesOne laptop runs Change Marker: mark and date every change. Save work every 15 minutes.
  • SourcesPhones or laptops search X, Telegram and Google.
  • SheetOne person keeps the group's Google Sheet: one row per change.

The mission in five steps

The pictures show where the ground changed and between which dates. A source says what happened. A row can have both.

  1. 1

    Detonations: when did each building go?

    • Our village pictures, layer True colour: Before 2026-02-28, After 2026-10-23.
    • Kind Building destroyed, then Area: draw each building or group of buildings that is gone. At 10 m a house is two or three pixels.
    • Select a mark. Step its After picture back month by month until the building is back, then forward one, and press First seen on this After picture: its Seen between gets the two dates.
    • One row per mark in the tab Detonations: Copy for the sheet, paste into Shape (GeoJSON), type the ID and Seen between. The Timeline tab counts your rows per interval.

    A picture cannot tell a demolition from an airstrike: only a source can. Fill How, as a source says, or pick Not stated.

  2. 2

    Fires: burned ground and heat from space

    • Layer Short-wave infrared (burns): a fresh burn scar is dark red to black where plants were green. Scars fade within weeks, so step through the months. Kind Burn scar, Area.
    • NASA FIRMS: heat spots seen by satellites, one month per link. In its table choose UTC, then add 3 hours from 2026-03-29 (2 hours before).
    • EFFIS: burnt areas mapped by Copernicus. An empty map may mean the service is down, not that nothing burned. Write what you checked, even nothing, in FIRMS and EFFIS.
    • A scar or a heat spot shows where and when, never what lit the fire: fill Lit by, as a source says, only from a source.

  3. 3

    Bulldozing: forest and farmland cleared

    • Layer False colour (plants red): Before 2025-10-21, After 2026-10-23. The same season, so a harvest does not look like a change.
    • Mark plants that are gone and still gone months later: Bulldozed land as an Area, New road or track as a Line.
    • Not a burn scar: check the patch in Short-wave infrared (burns). Not rubble: check True colour. Cannot tell? Trees or plants lost, in the tab Other changes.
    • Land before: what grew there, from the before picture. Area (ha) and Length (m): a number only.

    Ploughing also leaves bare soil: a field bare in both pictures is not a change.

  4. 4

    White phosphorus: start from our reports, place one

    • Below, from our records: reports of white phosphorus at Arnoun, each with its day, its village and its posts. Geolocate at least one of them.
    • From a burn scar: in Change Marker, layer Short-wave infrared (burns), set Before and After to the two pictures the report names. A dark patch that is not on the Before picture is a place to check.
    • From a heat spot: each report's NASA FIRMS link opens that day and the next, centred on the village. A video or photo helps when it shows a ridge, a building or a road to match on Google Earth. Shadow and smoke against SunCalc and the wind go in Sun and wind.
    • Then look for more reports with the searches in step 5. The pictures cannot identify the munition: write how you placed it in Located from, and a fire that followed in Fire after it.

  5. 5

    Sources: what the reports say

    • The searches for Arnoun are ready below. X search needs a signed-in X account, your own. Google has the dates set.
    • Read where each post puts the event: daily bulletins list many villages, and names repeat.
    • The time of a post is not the time of the event. Date (Beirut): an hour only if a source says when it happened.
    • Every link in Sources and media, the first post first. Two outlets that copy each other, or one outlet on two platforms, count as one source.
